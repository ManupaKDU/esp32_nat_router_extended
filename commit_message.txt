🎨 Palette: Add autofocus to lock and unlock forms

💡 What: Added the `autofocus` attribute to the primary password inputs in both the lock and unlock pages.
🎯 Why: These are single-action forms (either locking or unlocking the interface). Adding autofocus immediately places the user's cursor in the input field, reducing friction and making the interaction smoother.
📸 Before/After: N/A - non-visual UX improvement.
♿ Accessibility: Improves keyboard accessibility by correctly routing focus upon page load.
